Travel Experiences in Switzerland

Glacier Express

Duration: 8–8.5 hours

Step into one of the most beautiful train rides in the world, the Glacier Express, known as the “slowest express train.” You’ll glide through dramatic alpine valleys, 91 tunnels, and over 291 bridges. Snow-covered peaks, deep gorges, and charming Swiss villages make every moment feel cinematic.

Hiking in the Swiss Alps

Duration: 2 hours to 8 hours

Walk through breathtaking alpine meadows, crystal-clear streams, and snowy peaks in the iconic Swiss Alps. Every trail feels like a postcard, with fresh mountain air and peaceful silence around you.

Château de Chillon

Duration: 1.5–2 hours

Explore the magical lakeside fortress Château de Chillon, located on Lake Geneva. Walk through ancient stone halls, underground dungeons, and medieval towers that feel frozen in time.

Lindt Home of Chocolate

Duration: 1.5–2.5 hours

Enter a chocolate lover’s paradise at the Lindt & Sprüngli experience center. Discover how Swiss chocolate is made, watch giant chocolate fountains, and enjoy unlimited tasting moments.

Zoo Zurich

Duration: 2–4 hours

Visit the world-class Zoo Zürich, home to over 360 species. Walk through rainforest domes, penguin enclosures, and open natural habitats designed for animal comfort.

BearPark Bern

Duration: 1–1.5 hours

Meet the symbolic bears of Bern at BearPark Bern, located beside the Aare River. Watch bears roam freely in a natural riverside habitat while enjoying panoramic views of the old city.

Cable Car Mountain Rides

Duration: 30 minutes to 2 hours

Soar above valleys and forests in scenic cable cars reaching famous peaks like Mount Titlis or Jungfraujoch regions. Every ride offers jaw-dropping views of glaciers, lakes, and alpine villages.

Boating on Swiss Lakes

Duration: 1–3 hours

Relax on peaceful cruises across crystal-clear lakes like Lake Geneva or Lake Lucerne. Surrounded by mountains and charming towns, it’s a calming way to see Switzerland from the water.
